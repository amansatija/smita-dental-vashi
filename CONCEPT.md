# Smita Dental Care Centre — TEZ-010 Concept

## Positioning
**Trusted family dental care in Vashi Sec-17** — a calm, premium clinic with a 25+ year legacy, newly relocated to Shiv Center Building, for generations of Navi Mumbai families.

## One-liner
Neighbourhood-rooted dentistry in the heart of Vashi: ethical, appointment-led care from Dr Gaurang Mistry & Dr Raina Mistry, with Call / WhatsApp as the booking path.

## Visual direction
- **Palette:** Rosewood (`#6b2d45`) + soft blush (`#f0c9d0`) on warm cream — calm premium family clinic, deliberately distinct from Bright Smile’s teal, Ishravati’s warm indigo, Ingole’s sage/mint, and Shivam’s navy/gold.
- **Typography:** Cormorant Garamond (headings) + Outfit (UI/body) — editorial warm elegance.
- **Photography:** Professional clinic / consultation / operatory photos (Unsplash) — no cartoon teeth, no toys, no invented doctor portraits.
- **Motion:** Soft scroll reveals, subtle ken-burns on hero; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Differentiating layout idea
**“Calm care, Vashi trust”** — hero consultation photo with Sector-17 eyebrow → trust rail (Since 1999 · Specialists · New clinic · Mon–Sat) → photo service cards (Smile · Whitening · Implants · Full mouth) → doctor strip (text-led) → dual CTA banner → map + sticky Call/WhatsApp. Replaces their dated site’s weak conversion and buried CTAs.
